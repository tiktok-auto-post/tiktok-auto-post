Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TikTok Auto Post ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our service to automatically publish videos to your TikTok account.

1. Information We Collect

  • TikTok Account Information: When you connect your TikTok account, we access basic profile information (such as your username and profile picture) and the permissions you explicitly grant (such as the ability to upload and publish videos on your behalf).
  • Video Content: The video files, captions, and metadata you submit through our app for the purpose of publishing to your TikTok account.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical information such as log data, timestamps, and error reports to help us maintain and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your TikTok account via TikTok's official OAuth process.
  • To upload and publish videos to your TikTok account on your behalf, as instructed by you.
  • To troubleshoot issues and improve the reliability of the app.

We do not sell your personal information or video content to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We only share data with TikTok, as required to provide the auto-posting service, through TikTok's official API. We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Access tokens are stored securely and are only used to perform actions you have explicitly authorized.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see Section 8).

6. Your Rights

  • You may disconnect your TikTok account from our app at any time via TikTok's app permissions settings or by contacting us.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
